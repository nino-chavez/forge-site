# Forge Site's role in the Work Library

## Decision

Use Forge Site as the delivery playbook for a judged website surface. Do not use
it as the authority for the knowledge published on that surface.

Forge Site helps answer how a client or stakeholder site should be discovered,
designed, assembled, and handed off. Work Library decides which source-owned
artifacts enter the portal, at which revision, lifecycle, clearance, and route.

## What Forge Site contributes

The repository is a knowledge artifact rather than an application runtime. It
organizes website work around:

1. reconnaissance of the real client situation;
2. diagnosis against reusable business archetypes;
3. selection of proven implementation modules;
4. agent-assisted renovation under the client's brand and constraints; and
5. an operating handoff the client can use after delivery.

Its archetypes, modules, templates, and helper script reduce reinvention. They
do not make a generic site appropriate for every audience.

## How it applies here

Work Library can reuse Forge Site's encounter and delivery practices:

- identify who lands on a surface and what they decide there;
- inspect the source brand and prior art before selecting visual direction;
- make every card explain who it is for, what it contains, and what happens
  next;
- build recovery, empty, restricted, and stale states deliberately; and
- hand the finished surface back to an owner with a reproducible source path.

The Work Library portal remains a standalone product. It is not another Forge
Site archetype, and Forge Site does not own publication manifests, source
receipts, case lifecycle, or access control.

## Evidence boundary

The repository states that its patterns were extracted from shipped projects
and points to their locations. That makes it useful prior art. It is not a
controlled benchmark showing that every archetype or module improves delivery
speed, quality, or client outcomes.
